ABOUT THE TECHNOLOGY

Open AI. Your workflow.

GigDraft uses Flan-T5-small through Transformers.js. After its files are downloaded, text generation runs in your browser without a paid hosted text-generation API.

First use requires internet. Cached files may help with later use, but this version does not guarantee fully offline operation. The model is compact, so always review its output.
